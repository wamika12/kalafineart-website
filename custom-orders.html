How a Custom Order Works

  1. Share your idea

    Send me a few favorite photos and tell me who or what you’d like painted: a pet, your family, your wedding venue, or a milestone moment.

  2. Choose your style

    Together we pick the size, medium and look, from loose watercolor to finely detailed portraiture, plus any hidden “Easter eggs” you’d like woven in.

  3. I paint it by hand

    Every piece is painted by hand in my Seattle-area studio, with the same care I bring to live wedding paintings.

  4. Ready to gift or hang

    Your finished piece is carefully packaged and ready to give, frame or hang at home.

Popular as anniversary, wedding, holiday and memorial gifts, and as one-of-a-kind keepsakes of a beloved pet.
