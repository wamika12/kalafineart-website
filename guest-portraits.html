Guest Portraits

Live wedding and event guest portraits in Seattle and across Washington

  • Tailored Stationery: Drawn on custom, heavy-weight paper designed to match your wedding’s color palette and visual theme.
  • Flexible Styles: Options ranging from loose watercolor washes and detailed zoomed-in portraits to full-body fashion sketches and shimmering glitter accents.
  • The Ultimate Favor: A live, interactive experience that leaves every guest with a one-of-a-kind frameable keepsake from your celebration.

How it works

1. Custom Stationery Design

Before your big day I create custom stationery featuring your names, wedding date, and a frame color matched to your palette, setting the tone for the portraits to come.

2. On-Site Setup

I set up at your venue, wherever works best for your flow — cocktail hour, reception entrance, or a dedicated portrait corner.

3. Live Guest Portraits

As guests arrive, I photograph them and begin their custom portrait right there at the event.

4. Same-Day Pickup

Finished pieces are displayed at my booth throughout the celebration, so guests can pick up their portrait before they leave.

5. Shipped With Care

Any portraits not picked up, or finished after the event, are carefully packaged and shipped to you.
