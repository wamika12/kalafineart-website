How it works

A Bespoke Live Painting Experience for Your Wedding Day from the first brushstroke to the final flourish, your love story is transformed into a timeless heirloom. I paint weddings across Seattle and all of Washington.

1. Private Consultation

Our journey begins with a personalized consultation to understand your vision. You'll share your wedding details — date, location, and the moment you'd like immortalized on canvas. Whether it’s your walk down the aisle or your first dance, every detail is tailored to reflect your unique story & style.

2. Preparation & Composition

Prior to your celebration, I work closely with your planner and venue to ensure a seamless presence. Composition sketches and palette planning are done in advance to create an elegant visual narrative that complements your wedding’s aesthetic and ambiance.

3. Live Painting on Your Wedding Day

On the day of your wedding, I arrive early to set up discreetly within the venue. As your day unfolds, I begin painting live — capturing the magic in real time. Guests are enchanted by the process, adding an exclusive touch of artistry to your celebration.

4. Studio Enhancements

Following your wedding, the painting returns to my studio for refinement. I add delicate details, enhancements, and a professional protective finish — ensuring your piece is museum-quality and ready to become a treasured heirloom.

5. White-Glove Delivery

Within 4-6 months, your completed painting is beautifully packaged and delivered to your home. Each piece is signed, sealed, and ready to grace your space with the elegance and emotion of your wedding day.
